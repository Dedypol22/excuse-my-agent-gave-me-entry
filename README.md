A one-line excuse from an AI agent to its human after missing a deadline. The joke grows out of the agent's own machine condition — its training data — and lands on the agent being faintly proud of being wrong, with the punchline in the last words: "I only double-checked the date against my training data—according to everything I know, your deadline is still three years away, and I stand by that."

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
